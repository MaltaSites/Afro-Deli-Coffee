About

A neighborhood restaurant with genuine roots.

Afro Deli & Coffee is positioned as a traditional Ethiopian restaurant in Gzira, with a menu centered on East African dishes and a welcoming dine-in experience.

The public presence highlights authentic Ethiopian flavors, outdoor seating, and a social atmosphere that includes Afrobeats weekends and event-night energy.

About

A local spot with Ethiopian roots and an upbeat social rhythm.

Afro Deli & Coffee is publicly described as serving authentic Ethiopian flavors and traditional Ethiopian dishes in Gzira, pairing classic plates with a lively, community-oriented restaurant setting.

The restaurant also promotes Afrobeats every weekend, which gives the space a stronger evening identity than a standard deli or café.

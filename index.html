Our Roots

Located in the heart of Gzira, Afro Deli & Coffee brings the soulful and vibrant spirit of Africa to Malta. Inspired by traditional recipes passed down through generations, we serve dishes bursting with flavor, color, and history.

From our rich, hand-roasted African coffee to our hearty traditional meals, every bite tells a story of culture and community.

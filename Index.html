About Afro Deli & Coffee

Serving authentic Ethiopian flavors in Gzira, we bring a taste of tradition to your table. Our menu highlights East & West African dishes, slow‑cooked sauces, spiced stews, and fresh injera paired with bold, locally roasted coffee.

Open daily from 11:00 to 23:00, we host Afrobeats every weekend with DJs and live music, creating a cozy yet lively atmosphere for dinner, drinks, and late‑night vibes. Hours may run later on event nights.

  • Traditional Ethiopian dishes
  • East & West African flavors
  • Vegetarian & vegan options
  • Hosted Afrobeats nights
  • Dine‑in, takeaway & delivery
